Triangle Numbers Consider a triangle composed of numbers where the top of the triangle is 1. Each subsequent number in the triangle is equal to the sum of three numbers above it: its top-left number, its top number, and its top-right number. If any of these three numbers don’t exist, assume they are equal to 0.

Given a value representing a row of this triangle, return the position of the first even number in this row. Assume that the first number in each row is at position 1.

Then, we can iterate through the nth row until we encounter the first even number. However, this approach is inefficient because it results in an excessive use of time and memory to build the entire triangle. To find a more optimal solution, let’s consider how we can simplify the representation of our triangle.
